Our Journey Began with a Simple Dream

Founded on the belief that everyday moments deserve a touch of luxury, Liliyum Patisserie started in a small kitchen filled with the aroma of roasting cocoa beans and fresh vanilla pods.

We set out to create a sanctuary for dessert lovers—a place where traditional techniques meet contemporary flavors, resulting in pastries that are not just beautiful, but deeply comforting.
